# Surviving V2 exploratory work

The local workspace contained an uncommitted recent-tail grid and two missed-opportunity audits. They are preserved here because they record questions investigated after the initial rewrite.

The [tail-grid instrument](../v2/tools/research-lowtax-tail-grid.ts) folds a supplied local tape and scores alternative exits. It overrides the policy configuration during its fold. Its results are retrospective configured-policy experiments, not reproductions of historical live admissions. It has no execution driver. No new grid was run for publication.

The [missed-opportunity audit](../datasets/exploratory/miss-audit.json) and [open-window variant](../datasets/exploratory/miss-open.json) retain the reported numerical fields with identities pseudonymized. Their complete producing-command provenance and independent certification are unavailable. In particular, a field called missed winners is not proof that the bot could have captured those returns.

Original files and available tapes remain private. These exploratory outputs are not used in the public realized-cash tables or demo.
